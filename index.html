How it works

Three steps, and the first one is free.

  1. We look at what you've got

    An hour on site, no obligation, scheduled at a time that works for you — usually an evening or a weekend. We document what's running, what's failing quietly, and what's a genuine risk to the business. You get the findings in writing whether you hire us or not.

  2. We fix and standardise

    We clean up what's broken, verify the backups actually restore and patch what's exposed. It happens evenings and weekends, so nobody loses a working day to us.

  3. We keep it running

    Occasional help is billed hourly. Once support becomes a regular thing, we'll scope a flat monthly package instead, so you're not counting hours. Bigger projects get a fixed quote before we start, never a running tab.
